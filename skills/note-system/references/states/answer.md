# Answer State

Answer my question from the vault evidence established during discovery. The answer is the work;
do not edit the vault or turn a possible follow-up into unrequested vault changes.

Lead with the conclusion and distinguish what the vault states, what cited sources establish, and
what remains uncertain. Link relevant notes contextually. Use compact evidence rather than a vault
tour. If material evidence is missing, return to discovery instead of guessing.

Continue to completion when every material part of the question is answered and the response does
not depend on unsupported evidence or an unresolved judgment.
