# Source Preservation Procedure

Save what the source said separately from notes that interpret or combine its ideas.

## Save a faithful source note

Create one source note per source using the vault's template and location. Record useful source
details, such as identity, author, publication date, URL, transcript type, language, retrieval
date, and how the text was obtained or converted.

Keep the source body's wording, order, qualifications, examples, and useful timestamps
unchanged. Caption-to-Markdown conversion may remove formatting controls and confirmed
rolling-caption display repeats. Use timing and caption structure to identify text carried over
on screen; matching words alone do not justify removal. Keep genuine spoken repetition and cases
where the cause of repetition is unclear, and keep the raw caption evidence unchanged during
preparation.

Do not summarize, deduplicate spoken content, restructure, annotate inline, silently correct, or
revise the source body because later evidence is better. Label authored captions, platform
automatic captions, and local ASR accurately; generated text must not appear to be guaranteed
verbatim speech.

Keep sponsorships, repetition, filler, padding, and non-transferable anecdotes in the faithful
record. They may be ignored later when selecting knowledge. Backlinks from knowledge notes are
enough to reveal use; do not maintain a redundant derived-note inventory.

## Result

Include the source-note path, source identity, transcript origin, how timestamps link to the source,
temporary evidence paths, and any limits on transcript accuracy.
