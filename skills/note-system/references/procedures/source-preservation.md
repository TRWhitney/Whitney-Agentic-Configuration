# Source Preservation Procedure

Keep preserved source evidence separate from integrated knowledge.

## Preserve faithful evidence

Create one canonical source note per source. Follow the vault's established source template and
location. Metadata may normalize identity, author, publication date, source URL, transcript origin,
language, retrieval date, or processing provenance when those fields have a concrete use.

Preserve the source body's wording, order, qualifications, examples, and useful timestamps.
Caption-to-Markdown conversion may remove formatting controls and confirmed rolling-caption
display repeats. Use timing and caption structure to identify text carried over on screen; matching
words alone do not justify removal. Preserve genuine spoken repetition and ambiguous cases, and
keep the raw caption evidence unchanged during preparation.

Do not summarize, deduplicate spoken content, restructure, annotate inline, silently correct, or
revise the source body because later evidence is better. Label authored captions, platform automatic
captions, and local ASR accurately; generated text must not appear to be guaranteed verbatim speech.

Keep sponsorships, repetition, filler, padding, and non-transferable anecdotes in the faithful
record. They may be ignored later when selecting knowledge. Backlinks from knowledge notes are
enough to reveal use; do not maintain a redundant derived-note inventory.

## Result

Include the canonical source-note path, source identity, transcript origin, useful timestamp
mechanism, temporary evidence paths, and any fidelity limitation.
