# Verification Procedure

Verify changed definition notes whenever a task creates, corrects, consolidates, relinks, or
tags them. Research external sources only when it helps check a fact or explain a disagreement.
Verification supports note-taking; it must not overwhelm the source or turn routine vault work
into an open-ended research project.

Prefer primary sources, official documentation, academic research, direct expert sources, then
strong secondary sources. Treat weak anecdotes as weak evidence. A substantial independent
pattern may be summarized as an aggregate rather than elevating individual anonymous reports.

Compare the exact claim, conditions, date, and scope. Report important disagreements. Do not
silently change the saved source or decide a dispute for the reader. Useful verified knowledge
may enter the relevant concept note when clearly cited and distinguished from the original
source.

## Verify definition notes

Verify that each definition:

- has no YAML or properties unless I explicitly requested tags, in which case `tags` is its only
  property;
- has no `Related`, `See also`, or backlink-maintenance section;
- begins its body with the standard term as its title;
- gives a self-contained definition in one to three sentences;
- has at least one incoming link;
- has no competing definition note for a spelling or grammatical variant;
- is reached through display text where the surrounding sentence uses another form.

Read the note as a hover preview. Promote it to a concept note if understanding it requires
examples, argument, extensive qualifications, or navigation sections.

## Result

For external research, include the claims checked, sources consulted, supported conclusion or
discrepancy, confidence and scope, and the note passages the result may affect. For definition
review, include any failed checks and the affected notes.
