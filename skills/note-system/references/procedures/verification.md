# Verification Procedure

Perform bounded external research only when it practically improves factual accuracy or resolves a
material discrepancy. Verification supports note-taking; it must not overwhelm the source or turn
routine vault work into an open-ended research project.

Prefer primary sources, official documentation, academic research, direct expert sources, then
strong secondary sources. Treat weak anecdotes as weak evidence. A substantial independent pattern
may be summarized as an aggregate rather than elevating individual anonymous reports.

Compare the exact claim, conditions, date, and scope. Preserve meaningful discrepancies rather than
silently correcting the archived source or deciding a disputed issue for the reader. Useful verified
knowledge may enter the relevant concept note when clearly cited and distinguished from the
original source.

## Result

Include the claims checked, sources consulted, supported conclusion or discrepancy, confidence and
scope, and the note passages the result may affect.
