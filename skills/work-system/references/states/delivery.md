# Delivery State

Turn validated work into a complete, durable repository outcome. Delivery owns reconciliation with
durable knowledge, independent review of the complete work, and preservation in repository history.
It does not own corrections to accepted intent, implementation, or validation evidence.

## Assemble the complete work

Begin with the validated implementation, tests, accepted artifacts, and current completion evidence.
Include every task-owned change that belongs to the completed outcome.

Resolve documentation impact and update any durable work record before review. Documentation,
completion records, and other artifacts expected in the result must be present before the complete
work is reviewed.

Do not use documentation, review, or commit work to conceal an implementation defect, evidence gap,
or unresolved decision.

## Adjudicate delivery results

Review the complete work only after its delivery artifacts are current. Classify every failed
procedure or validated finding by the work responsible for resolving it:

- A documentation, review-execution, history-shaping, or commit problem remains in delivery.
- Missing, stale, or inadequate completion evidence belongs to validation.
- Incorrect, incomplete, or unsafe behavior belongs to implementation.
- Misunderstood intent, missing acceptance criteria, or an unresolved consequential decision
  belongs to the earliest available state that can correct it.

Use the corresponding available route rather than correcting earlier work in delivery. Preserve
results that remain valid. After any correction, repeat validation and delivery work whose evidence,
scope, or conclusions may have changed.

## Preserve the result

Commit only after documentation is resolved, every required review axis passes, and completion
evidence still applies to the complete work. Include its accepted source artifacts and current work
record in the complete outcome; do not commit them separately in an earlier state. A commit blocker
leaves the work in delivery until it is resolved.

When more accepted work remains in the active workflow, use the available continuation route after
the current outcome is delivered. Complete the workflow only when no accepted work remains and its
durable record is current.

## Continue

Continue when documentation is resolved, review passes without an unresolved finding, required
commits exist, completion evidence remains valid, and no decision or correction belongs to an
earlier state.
