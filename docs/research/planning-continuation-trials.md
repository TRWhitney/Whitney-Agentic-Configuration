## Method

The applied [private criterion](compaction-feedback-coverage-proposal.diff) checks continuation
after answers and corrections, including before compaction. Requests, supplied files and other
criteria are unchanged. Six fresh baseline attempts precede eighteen isolated trial attempts,
three per case/control. Both use gpt-6.1-sol candidates and separate blinded judges, 1800/1200-second
allowances and disabled token guards. A further blinded comparison judges each baseline alongside
all three trial outputs under shuffled labels. Same-family judging remains a limitation.
